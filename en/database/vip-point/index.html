Privilege Roulette Overview

Basic Structure
  • After reaching the maximum VIP level, remaining VIP points can be used for Privilege Roulette spins.
  • Special rewards in the Privilege Roulette, including King of Games Fragments and Player One Fragments, are removed from the roulette after the related puzzle is completed.
  • These are probability-based rewards, and participating or purchasing does not guarantee a specific item.
Event Rules
  • There are two puzzle types: King of Games and Player One. Completing a puzzle grants the corresponding skin.
  • To light up a puzzle piece, you must obtain the matching puzzle fragment.
  • Puzzle fragments and privilege badges are roulette rewards, and privilege badges can be used in the Privilege Shop.
  • Items in the Privilege Shop refresh every Monday at 00:00 UTC.
  • Skins can only be exchanged once. After being exchanged, they will no longer appear after the next refresh.

Special Item Probability

King of Games Fragments + Player One Fragments

The base probability for the special item combination is 0.03%. The final combined probability, including pity protection, is listed as 1.33%. For the first 10 King of Games Fragments, you are guaranteed 1 within 80 spins. After obtaining 11 or more, you are guaranteed 1 within 100 spins.
