Snowball: effects, training unlocks and recommended heroes

What effects does Snowball provide?

Snowball — Striking Tide, skill level 1: The Baby Seal kicks up a wave and launches a surprise attack. At the start of battle, deals Water Damage equal to 500% of Pet Attack to 1 random enemy Hero.

The training stage shown unlocks the skill; it is not the pet level or the server opening date.
