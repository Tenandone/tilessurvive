Pet System

The Pet System lets Chiefs obtain Pet Eggs from Pet-related Intel Missions, hatch and raise Pets, and use them to support Hero teams.

Quick Facts

AvailabilityGradual rollout based on State progress
How to obtain PetsComplete Pet-related Intel Missions to obtain Pet Eggs
Last Verified2026-09-18

Growth

Hatch Pets, raise them, name them, and unlock different appearances

Team support

Raised Pets can grant stats to all Heroes and support teams in battle

Availability depends on State progress. Check the live game before planning time-sensitive upgrades.
