Promotion Requirement

Season promotion heroes are unlocked during season play, and require a 10-star Epic SR hero as promotion material.

Fragment Cost Rule

After promotion, star upgrades require Epic Hero Fragments or that hero’s own fragments, and the fragment cost is applied at double the normal rate.
