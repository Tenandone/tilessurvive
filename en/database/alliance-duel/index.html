Participation Requirements

  1. Commanders with Power Plant level 10 or higher can participate in the Alliance Duel event.
  2. Alliances with 10 or more members can enter Alliance Duel matchmaking.
  3. Only alliances ranked in the top 32 on their server can enter the next week's Alliance Duel matchmaking on Sunday.

Event Explanation

  1. Your alliance faces one enemy alliance in a week-long duel event. During the event, each day has a theme, and alliance members can earn duel points by completing actions that match that day’s theme. The alliance with more Alliance Duel points on that day earns the daily victory point.
  2. The alliance with more victory points over the full week wins that week's duel.
  3. If you change alliances during the event period, your duel points for that day will not be counted toward alliance points. Your personal points are not affected, and you can still open personal point chests.
  4. You can only earn points by purchasing packages that include diamonds.
