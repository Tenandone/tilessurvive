Nuclear Age Manual

Nuclear upgrades take your settlement beyond its previous building limits. Prepare your fuel supply and choose each upgrade step with your next goal in mind.

Availability follows your State’s progress. Check the unlock notice in your game.

Entering the Nuclear Age

Your Power Plant must reach level 30, and the feature must be available in your State.

Availability follows your State’s progress. Check the unlock notice in your game.

What to prepare first

Start producing Nuclear Fuel, check the required buildings, and compare the materials and time for your next upgrades.

Check the in-game refund conditions before canceling an upgrade.
