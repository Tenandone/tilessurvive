Eva

SR Melee

Eva is an SR melee hero in Tiles Survive. As a Rover faction hero, she specializes in sustained firepower, increased gathering speed, and strong base stat boosts.

At max level and stars, Eva provides March Capacity +20.0K, Hero Attack +40.11K, Hero Defense +8.02K, and Hero Health +2.67M.

Hero Story

Eva is the iron fist of the slums. With a washed-up boxer for a father and a mother who died young, she learned by age ten that survival requires bloodied knuckles.

Her undefeated record in the underground circuit soon caught the attention of the mob. While the mobsters called her Wasp as she stood over her victims in the casino, they did not know she was using her cut to buy punching bags for the neighborhood gym.

And she never hesitates to use a gun when challenged. I can knock some sense into you, but bullets always work better.
