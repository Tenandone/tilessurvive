Hero Story

Travis is a strange hunter. He is cold and quiet with people but respects every animal he hunts, believing that taking a life means honoring it and ensuring that no part of the kill goes to waste.

Raised by his grandfather, he first fired a gun at age 8, shattering a lightbulb, and he has been obsessed with hunting ever since. A fellow hunter accidentally shot and killed his grandfather on a hunt. A few months later, that hunter had an accident of his own.

Travis became a professional hunter, but there is one more species on his list of prey than most other hunters.
