Chef

SR Melee

Chef is an SR melee hero in Tiles Survive. He belongs to the Mountain faction and combines pot attacks, self-healing, enemy taunt effects, and a Greenhouse production speed boost.

At max level and stars, Chef provides March Capacity +20.0K, Hero Attack +40.11K, Hero Defense +8.02K, and Hero Health +2.67M.

Hero Story

Raised by the community, Chef was bullied as a child for his weight. He was adopted by Nadia, a spirited chef who discovered his talent by accident. After training, he became a master chef.

He also learned how to pilot boats and hot-air balloons while working as a head chef in floating and traveling restaurants.

During the doomsday, everyone was starving, but Chef somehow never lost a pound. In a world like this, that may not be such a bad thing after all.
