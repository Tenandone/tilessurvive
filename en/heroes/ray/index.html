Ray

SSR Ranged

Ray is an SSR ranged hero in Tiles Survive. As a Stalwart faction hero, he specializes in explosive bombardment, chained burst damage, eagle-assisted follow-up attacks, and strong sustained single-target pressure.

At max level and stars, Ray provides March Capacity +20.0K, Hero Attack +64.38K, Hero Defense +12.87K, and Hero Health +4.29M.

Hero Story

Ray grew up in the slums, battling hunger and violence before he was 14. After joining a gang, his cunning and ruthlessness made him a mob boss.

When the virus struck and his empire fell apart, Ray lit a cigar and sneered, Time to start over. Then, without breaking a sweat, he rescued his most loyal subordinate, Rolex.

Ray is not the kind of man who stays in the back and waits for the fight to end. With calculated timing and overwhelming firepower, he crushes enemies from range and keeps pressuring the same target until it falls.
