Maddie

SSR Backline

Maddie is an SSR backline hero in Tiles Survive. She belongs to the Aeronaut faction and specializes in single-target shots, wide-area flame attacks, healing support, and boosting her own combat stats.

At max level and stars, Maddie provides March Capacity +20.0K, Hero Attack +64.38K, Hero Defense +12.87K, and Hero Health +4.29M.

Hero Story

Maddie had a happy childhood until a camping trip at age 12, when she lost her teddy bear. Her father was impaled on a rebar while searching for it that night and died of tetanus because he couldn't get treatment in time.

Her mother remarried a younger man whom Maddie distrusted, so she devoted herself to archery to get her mother's attention.

After the Outbreak, Maddie carries her old teddy bear on her back as she protects her mother and Frank with her arrows. I won't lose anyone this time.
