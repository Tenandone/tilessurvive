Hero atlas

Lava

StalwartX-SSRGuard

Official notes describe attacks that can inflict Burning, faster attacks during Rampage, and a switch between extra damage and healing through attacks as Health changes.

SkillsRecommended petsGrowth

Skills

01

Claw Combo

Deals damage with a series of attacks, with a chance to inflict Burning for 3 seconds.

02

Molten Rampage

Attacks nearby enemies and goes Berserk for 5 seconds. While Berserk, Attack Speed increases and Normal Attacks deal extra damage to Burning enemies.

03

Molten Duality

Above 50% Health, Auto Attack Damage increases. Below 50%, Normal Attack hits restore Health.

04

Molten Heart

Increases all of their own stats.
