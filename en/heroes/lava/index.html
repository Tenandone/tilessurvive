Hero atlas

Lava

StalwartX-SSRGuard

Released · State unlocks vary

Availability follows your State’s hero release schedule. Check Hero Recruitment in your game.

Official notes describe attacks that can inflict Burning, faster attacks during Rampage, and a switch between extra damage and healing through attacks as Health changes.

SkillsGrowthExclusive gearRecommended pets

Skills

1 · Maximum level 50

Claw Combo

Swings the Lava Claws in a brutal combo. Each hit deals damage equal to 35% of Attack and has a 25% chance to inflict Burning (deals damage equal to 50% of Attack per second) for 3 seconds.

2 · Maximum level 50

Molten Rampage

“Let my rage erupt like molten fury!” Roars and shatters the ground, dealing damage equal to 800% of Attack to nearby enemies and entering a Berserk status for 5 seconds. While Berserk, Attack Speed increases by 100%. Normal Attacks that hit Burning targets deal 15% additional damage.

3 · Maximum level 50

Molten Duality

When Health is above 50%, Auto Attack Damage increases by 7.5%. When Health is below 50%, each Normal Attack hit restores Health equal to 100% of Attack.

4 · Maximum level 5

Molten Heart

Lava's all Stats +20%.
