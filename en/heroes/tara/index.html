Tara

SSR Midline

Tara is an SSR midline hero in Tiles Survive. She belongs to the Stalwart faction and combines area damage, heavy front pressure, and full-team defense support.

At max level and stars, Tara provides March Capacity +20.0K, Hero Attack +64.38K, Hero Defense +12.87K, and Hero Health +4.29M.

Hero Story

Tara stood barefoot at the cliff's edge, her meteor hammer sweeping a silver arc through the dusk as feathers and strands of her hair swayed in the wind. Raised by the natives, she had learned to breathe like the wolves.

When intruders stormed the village, demanding the dancing girl, Tara answered with a crack of her weapon, and the white wolf by her side had already torn out the first trespasser's throat.

I'd rather be with animals. Flicking the blood from her skirt, Tara vanished into the misty pines and became a feared guardian who fights beside wolves on the battlefield.
