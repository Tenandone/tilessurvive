Hero Details

Rosie

SSR Melee

Rosie is an SSR melee hero in Tiles Survive. She belongs to the Mountain faction and is a frontline damage dealer built around sonic strikes, area damage, self-healing, and strong staying power in battle.

At max level and stars, Rosie provides March Capacity +20.0K, Hero Attack +64.38K, Hero Defense +12.87K, and Hero Health +4.29M.

Hero Story

Before the apocalypse, Rosie had a dream of forming a rock band. The stage that once looked dazzling and glamorous has now fallen apart, much like the life of a girl born into poverty.

She had nothing but music, yet she used it to stand against the whole world. By the time she finally reached the stage, the apocalypse had already begun, with sickness and slaughter everywhere.

Even her parents died on their way to see her performance. This apocalypse is absurd, and the fluffy teddy bear is only a toy, but the bloodstained guitar is no decoration. Like the fiercest song, Rosie rampages forward and never backs down.
