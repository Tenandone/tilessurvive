Skills and effects

v2.6.200 · 2026-10-09

Hydro Pump

Korean in-game name: 호스 분사

Sprays water at the target. The stat used as the basis of the displayed damage coefficient is not specified.

Cooldown: 1 s

Hydro Barrage

Korean in-game name: 하이드로 물대포

Fires 3 water bombs. Has a 50% chance to reduce the target’s ATK and DEF by 3% each for 3 seconds. This reduction can stack.

Cooldown: 9 s

The table preserves the damage coefficient shown in the skill description. Whether it is per bomb or the combined total is unspecified; no stacking limit is given.

Chilling Water

Korean in-game name: 완만한 물줄기

Reduces ATK and DEF for all enemies.

Deep Dive Protection

Korean in-game name: 심해 잠수 보호

Unlocks at 8 stars. Increases all of the hero’s attributes by 20%. This is a preview of the locked specialty.
