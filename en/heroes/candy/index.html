Hero Detail

Candy

SSR Mid Range

Candy is an SSR mid-range hero in Tiles Survive. As a Rover faction hero, she specializes in single-target shots, team healing, enemy-wide Attack reduction, and self-stat enhancement, making her a support-focused hero.

At max level and stars, Candy provides March Capacity +20.0K, Hero Attack +64.38K, Hero Defense +12.87K, and Hero Health +4.29M.

Hero Story

I'm sorry, but my heart belongs to someone else. By her first year of high school, Candy had already turned down 36 confessions with the same gentle smile. At school, she was known as a top student, kind, calm, and seemingly flawless.

But no one knew how obsessed she was with horror movies, or that she had been practicing archery and horse riding for years. Even when she broke her leg while skiing, she only smiled and treated it like nothing serious.

When the Infected outbreak exploded, she was stranded in a foreign red-light district as an exchange student and was rescued by Black Wasp Eva. She adapted to wasteland life with frightening speed and learned how to shoot from Eva. Her hidden craving for danger turned out to be exactly what she needed to survive.
