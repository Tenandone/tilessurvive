Hero Detail

Skills

Deadshot
Lv. 40 Battle

Chiron fires a lethal arrow, dealing damage equal to 139% of Attack to an enemy target. This attack gains +25 Crit if the target's Defense is reduced.

Arrowstorm
Lv. 40 Battle

Chiron fires a volley of arrows that rain down, dealing piercing damage equal to 1,390% of Attack to all enemies in range and reducing their Defense by 10% for 5 seconds.

Piercing Resonance
Lv. 40 Support

Draws power from the bowstring's resonance, increasing all allies' Attack by 2.78% and Defense by 2.78%.

Hawk-Eye, Iron-Arm
Lv. 4 Passive

Boosts hero's all stats by 50%.

Hero Story

Under the Olympic spotlight, 46-year-old Chiron draws an arrow and murmurs, Shh... quiet. The bowstring hums, and the bullseye splits beneath a spinning shaft.

That level of precision was forged in the wild during his childhood. He once shot a viper clean through the head to save a blue-crowned jay, and loosed an arrow faster than a poacher could pull the trigger.

In a world crawling with the Infected, his green cloak is his armor. Chiron still hunts the same way he always has: silent, steady, and deadly.
