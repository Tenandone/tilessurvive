Hero atlas

Furiosa

RoverX-SSRGunner

Released · State unlocks vary

Availability follows your State’s hero release schedule. Check Hero Recruitment in your game.

A Rover X-SSR hero who supports the front row. Ragehorn provides area damage and stuns, while her skills weaken enemy defense and strengthen frontline allies.

SkillsGrowthExclusive gearRecommended pets

Skills

1 · Maximum level 50

Deadly Barrage

Fires her sawed-off shotgun, dealing damage equal to 120% of Attack to enemies within the hit area. On hit, reduces the target's Defense by 10% for 2s and increases her Defense by 10% for 2s.

2 · Maximum level 50

Ragehorn Rampage

"My Ragehorn isn't just for joyrides!" Furiosa charges forward on Ragehorn, launching enemies in her path into the air with its horns and dealing damage equal to 350% of Attack. She then doubles back and spins twice at high speed, dealing damage equal to 650% of Attack to nearby enemies and Stunning them for 2s.

3 · Maximum level 50

Ride Together

Powered by her exclusive vehicle, Ragehorn, Furiosa's Max Health increases by 10%. At the start of battle, increases all front row Heroes' Attack by 2.5% for the entire battle.

4 · Maximum level 5

Wasteland Heart

Furiosa's all Stats +20%.
