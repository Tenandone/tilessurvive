Hero Detail

Tarzan

SSR Melee

Tarzan is an SSR melee hero in Tiles Survive. Belonging to the Mountain faction, he is a frontline tank-style hero built around diving forward, smashing enemies in an area, boosting his own Attack and Defense, and staying alive under pressure.

At max level and stars, Tarzan provides March Capacity +20.0K, Hero Attack +64.38K, Hero Defense +12.87K, and Hero Health +4.29M.

Hero Story

In a rusted cage reeking of blood and sweat, Tarzan twists and hammers an uppercut like a wrecking ball. Red mist bursts across the mesh as bones crack beneath the roar of the crowd.

Passion! Rage! Victory! Once a slave left to rot in the arena's shadows, he reforged his broken chains into iron knuckles and smashed his fate apart through two hundred brutal fights.

Tarzan is a survivor forged through pain and close combat. On the battlefield, he charges in first, holds the line, and keeps pushing forward as the Mountain faction's living shield.
