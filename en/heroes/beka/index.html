Hero Story

Becca's blonde hair is the color of sun-drenched wheat. She followed in the footsteps of her father, a DEA agent killed in action, and enrolled in the police academy.

She solved seven interstate serial murder cases, but was always chewed out for acting without authorization. After the Outbreak, Becca led rescue missions for refugees.

But as she tracked the source of the virus, she uncovered secrets about her own origins. Even then, she never wavered, bringing order to the battlefield with calm judgment and relentless enforcement.
