Nikola

SSR Melee

Nikola is an SSR melee hero in Tiles Survive. He belongs to the Mountain faction and excels as a defensive frontline hero with electric burst damage, strong shields, survival tools, and reliable frontline durability.

At max level and stars, Nikola provides March Capacity +20.0K, Hero Attack +64.38K, Hero Defense +12.87K, and Hero Health +4.29M.

Hero Story

Once a brilliant electrician, Nikola loved building strange gadgets from scrap. His wife would always complain, even as she patched his worn-out pants with bulletproof fabric.

When the outbreak began, he jury-rigged a stun gun from a wrench, only to watch his wife and daughter become infected. He was forced to kill his own family, and after his right arm was badly bitten, he replaced it with a cybernetic limb powered by electromagnetic energy.

When fully charged, it glows with a faint blue current. Carrying unbearable loss, Nikola now stands on the battlefield as the Mountain faction’s shield, enduring enemy assaults with electricity and layered barriers.
