Hero Details

Hero Introduction

Once a former special forces commander, Sarge is an iron-willed soldier whose elite combat skills inspire confidence.

Seven years ago, he turned to smuggling to save his terminally ill daughter. After being reported and discharged, he worked three jobs and spent his life savings to give her five more years. The day after her funeral, his wife disappeared.

He spent three years in a drunken haze, but when the Outbreak began, the steel warrior rediscovered his will to fight. He now forges a path to survival for others from a fortress he built among the ruins.
