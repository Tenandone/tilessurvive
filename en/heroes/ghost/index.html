Ghost

Rare Ranged

Ghost is a rare ranged hero in Tiles Survive. As a Rover faction hero, he controls enemies and stacks damage over time with special paint attacks and graffiti zones.

At max level and stars, Ghost provides March Capacity +20.0K, Hero Attack +25.79K, Hero Defense +5.15K, and Hero Health +1.71M.

Hero Story

Born into a family of artists, the rebellious Ghost has been obsessed with graffiti since childhood. He thrived in art school, where his work won numerous awards, though his unconventional style often drew criticism.

When civilization collapsed, this once-refined young man who used to spend time in art galleries and drinking champagne now roams the ruins with spray cans, painting blood-red roses on crumbling walls, determined to leave beauty in the twilight of the doomsday.
