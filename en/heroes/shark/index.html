Skills

Shark’s skills combine area damage, damage reduction, frontline support, and self stat growth.

Crashing Wave

Shark swings a giant anchor, then lands and deals attack-based area damage. The damage coefficient is 79.23%.

Shark Surge

Activates shark form, reduces incoming damage by 15%, and creates a water field under Shark. The area damage equals 115.37% of attack every second for 4 seconds.

Vanguard Support

Increases HP and DEF for all allied frontline heroes by 4.17% each during battle.

Ocean's Might

A passive-style effect that increases Shark’s own stats. The Lv.4 effect is all stats +50%.
