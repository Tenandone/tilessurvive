Tony

SSR Guards
Hero Details

Tony is an SSR Guards hero in Tiles Survive. He belongs to the Aeronaut faction and combines pulse pistol damage, area burst attacks, team defense support, and strong frontline survivability.

At max level and 10-star stats, Tony provides March Capacity +20.0K, Hero Attack +64.38K, Hero Defense +12.87K, and Hero Health +4.29M.

Hero Introduction

Under the spotlights, Tony's messy curls and lazy smile made him a beloved child star after a cameo at age seven. Backstage at awards shows, he'd rather play action games than listen to his manager—his sister—pitch campus dramas.

He played the perfect student for the cameras, but his real idol was Bruce Lee. He practiced his strikes until he was covered in bruises. During an argument with his manager, he shouted, I'm not your cash cow! He never knew she was the one secretly providing the medicine for his injuries.

On the day of the Outbreak, she pushed him to safety before being dragged into the Horde. Seeing her disappear into that sea of monsters was when he finally realized how much she loved him. Now, he fights through the apocalypse to keep his companions safe, honoring the memory of a love he only recognized when it was gone.
