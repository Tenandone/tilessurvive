Hero Introduction

Lucky was sent to an orphanage after her parents died in a car crash. There, she learned to get by smuggling candy and other contraband. While escaping the orphanage, Lucky got captivated by a circus show and became the dazzling star of the acrobatic stage.

But when she saw her fellow performers and animals abandoned after being injured, her new home became a nightmare.

When the Outbreak began, Sarge helped her escape. Now, she searches for a place to truly call home.
