Exclusive gear costs

Official v2.5.200: Ocean Vox Lv.10 was improved to grant an ATK boost to all allies when Undine casts her active skill. The notice does not specify the amount.

These are equipment-panel values. Lv.15 is the maximum-level preview, not the hero’s total stats. The panel does not separate any additional bonuses.

The exclusive skill grants allied front-row DEF and back-row ATK +10% each at Lv.1, or +30% each in the Lv.15 preview. From Lv.10, casting the combat skill gives all allies +7.5% ATK for the combat skill’s effect duration. Lv.15 adds +10% auto-attack speed and +10% auto-attack damage.
