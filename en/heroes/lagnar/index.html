Ragnar

SSR Sea · Melee

Ragnar is an SSR hero from the Sea faction. He leads melee troops in battle and combines Water Blade effects with warship support damage.

His kit combines single-target damage, area damage, attack reduction, and self-stat boosts for sustained pressure.

An SSR Sea melee hero built around Water Blade effects and warship support damage.

Skills

Ragnar main skill effects.

Water Blade Strike

Swings a water blade to deal 125.1% damage to the target. After every 2 attacks, the next attack releases Water Blade energy for larger area damage. It also reduces the target’s attack by 15% for 5 seconds.

Support Warship

Immediately sends a warship along the side of the battlefield, crushing enemies on its path and dealing 556% damage.

Unyielding Spirit

Increases Ragnar’s attack and defense by 13.9%.

Warforged

Increases all of Ragnar’s attributes by 50%.
