Hero Detail

Jacob is an SSR melee hero in Tiles Survive. He belongs to the Wasteland faction and specializes in sustained flame damage, area bomb pressure, enemy-wide Attack and Defense reduction, and disruptive frontline control.

Raging Flames
Lv. 40 Battle

Burn, toys! Jacob sprays flames in front with a flamethrower, dealing damage equal to 139% of Attack every 0.5 seconds. Each hit has a 10% chance to reduce enemy Defense by 10% for 3 seconds. This effect can stack.

Bomb Carnival
Lv. 40 Battle

Throws a bomb doll that taunts nearby enemies. The doll has 20% of the Hero's Health and explodes after 2 seconds, dealing damage equal to 1,390% of Attack to enemies in range.

Hero Story

Jacob was once a star comedian, dominating TV screens and theater stages with his manic energy and razor-sharp wit. But as the internet age took hold, his outrageous jokes were twisted into scandals, fueling controversy and forcing him out of the spotlight.

Rather than back down, he embraced the chaos, posing as a senator and turning congress into his deranged stage.

When the world fell, so did the curtains on his old life. He transformed his juggling balls into mini bombs, and for his grand finale hurled them into a horde of Infected, setting off a crimson fireworks display. Draped in a red trench coat and standing over scorched earth, he grinned as laughter and explosions filled the air. Wanna hear my latest joke?
