Hero Story

Born into privilege, Wright was a frail child, often bullied by aristocratic peers. To fight back, he learned slingshot crafting from his grandfather, igniting a passion for invention.

Idolizing Iron Man, he dreamed of becoming a genius inventor, but after his weapons accidentally harmed a dear friend, he abandoned that dream, at least for a while.

His world crumbled when his parents were assassinated over unpaid debts after their bankruptcy. Now a fugitive alongside his grandfather, Wright roams the post-apocalyptic wasteland, building weapons to survive while desperately searching for his missing guardian.
