Hero Story

Rusty grew up under the weight of his father's football dreams, with a stray dog named Pad as his only true friend. One day after school, his father ran over the dog, casually remarking, I didn't see him while backing up. Rusty didn't say a word. He just grabbed his gear and left for good.

While struggling on the field, Rusty fell for a gambling setup. Banned from the sport, he was forced into a gang, living a life of constant violence. After the Outbreak, Rusty's boss was eliminated in a power struggle. Tired of the criminal life, Rusty took his football and left that life behind.
