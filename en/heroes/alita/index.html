Hero atlas

Alita

AeronautX-SSRGunner

Coming soon · State unlocks vary

Announced as in development on October 30, 2026; a launch preview followed on November 3. Exact availability depends on your State.

An upcoming Aeronaut X-SSR hero. Bad Bunny Mk. I combines rapid lasers and carrot bombs with self-cleansing and resistance to control effects.

SkillsGrowthExclusive gearRecommended pets

Skills

1 · Maximum level 50

Laser Volley

English reference name

Korean original: 레이저 연사

Fires repeated lasers, dealing 100% of Attack. Has a 30% chance to raise her Attack by 10% for 2 seconds.

2 · Maximum level 50

Mega Carrot, Fire!

English reference name

Korean original: 메가 당근, 발사!

Deals 1200% of Attack as area damage. Immune to debuffs and control effects while casting.

3 · Maximum level 50

Firepower Tuning

English reference name

Korean original: 화력 조정

Raises her Crit by 15 for the entire battle.

4 · Maximum level 5

Genius Retrofit

English reference name

Korean original: 천재적인 개조

Increases all her stats by 20%.
