Buildings

Lab 1

Lab 1 researches technologies for combat and development. Upgrading can unlock more advanced technologies.

← All buildingsRequirements by levelPlanning and useRelated buildings and guides

Planning and use

Find the lab level required by your next technology. Choosing the development, economy or combat research you need gives the building a clear upgrade target.

Before upgrading

Lab upgrade costs and technology costs are separate. Do not apply the research-speed effect to base construction time.

Applicable conditions

The v2.6.200 research screen shows original time before speedups and bonuses. Lab building costs and individual research costs are separate. A complete table of research-node costs, maximum levels and prerequisite trees is not provided here.

Research Priority

All research can be completed over time, but it is still better to set early priorities first.

Top Priority

Infected Elimination

This research improves efficiency for rewards earned from defeating regular infected and infected bosses. It is worth prioritizing early because the gain in daily farming and speed-up items is noticeable.

Top Priority

Alliance Duel

This is a core research line directly tied to Alliance Duel scoring. The more consistently you invest in it, the better your alliance activity efficiency becomes.

Research costs and requirements

This page covers research effects and priorities. A complete table of node costs and prerequisites is not provided; check the in-game research screen for those requirements.

Infected Elimination Reference Data

Effects and power correspond to the level and conditions stated for each entry.

Infected Elimination - Speed-Up Item I

At level 5, defeating infected grants 5 speed-up items and 131100 power.

Boss Elimination - Speed-Up Item I

At level 5, defeating an infected boss grants 5 speed-up items and 348340 power.

Infected Elimination - Speed-Up Item II

At level 5, defeating infected grants 5 speed-up items and 485560 power.

Boss Elimination - Speed-Up Item II

At level 5, defeating an infected boss grants 5 speed-up items and 543120 power.

Random Crate

An extra random crate can drop from defeating regular infected, and the effect currently shows up to 100 obtainable per day.

Summary

Infected Elimination research is worth placing high in the early priority order because the daily farming reward impact is felt quickly.

Infected Fiend Rally reward conditions

Infected Fiend Conquest EXP Bonus and Speedup Items research bonuses apply only to the Rally leader after a successful defeat, not to Rally participants. (v2.5.500)
