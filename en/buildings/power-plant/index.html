Building

Power Plant

The Power Plant is the center of your Settlement and the foundation of building growth. Upgrading unlocks new buildings and upgrades.

Building List Database
← All buildingsRequirements by levelPlanning and useRelated buildings and guides

Planning and use

Check the Power Plant level required by your next building goal. Budget for the linked buildings separately as well as the Power Plant itself.

Before upgrading

Range totals exclude upgrades to prerequisite buildings. Also check the required buildings, including the Hospital, in your game.

Power Plant Skills

Skill effects and cost by level

Instant Production

Produces all resources equal to 900 minutes of your current resource production rate.

Cooldown 23 hours

Construction Boost

Reduces the remaining time of the building currently under construction or upgrade by 240 minutes.

Cooldown 23 hours

Enhanced Training

Reduces the training time of troops currently in training by 120 minutes.

Cooldown 23 hours

Focused Research

Reduces the research time of the technology currently being researched by 240 minutes.

Cooldown 23 hours
