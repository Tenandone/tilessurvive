Lookout Tower

The Lookout Tower has recorded level costs, time and linked prerequisites. Its detailed role and level effects remain unconfirmed.

Official representative image. It does not show changes in appearance by level.

← All buildingsRequirements by levelPlanning and useRelated buildings and guides

Planning and use

If a target building lists the Lookout Tower, review the costs to reach that level. For a feature-based upgrade decision, check the game’s description first.

Before upgrading

No scouting or defense function or unlock level is inferred from the name or image. Unconfirmed effects are not added to the cost table.
