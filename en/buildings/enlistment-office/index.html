Enlistment Office Rules

These are the core rules you should know when managing the Enlistment Office.

Recruitment and Loyalty Guide

  1. If troops die in battle without being able to enter the hospital, the Commander can recruit the same number of troops from the Enlistment Office.
  2. When troops become recruitable in the Enlistment Office, Loyalty is accumulated automatically, and this Loyalty can be used to recover the troops there.
  3. The Enlistment Office can only store a limited amount of Loyalty, and neither automatic accumulation nor direct acquisition can exceed the cap.
  4. The fewer wounded troops there are in the hospital, the faster Loyalty accumulates.
  5. As accumulated Loyalty gets closer to the maximum cap, the Loyalty accumulation rate slows down.
  6. The more troops available for recall, the faster Loyalty accumulates.
  7. When there are troops in the Enlistment Office, you can earn daily activity points and gain Loyalty while gathering resources.
  8. The higher the Furnace level, the more Loyalty you gain from activity points.

About Loyalty

This is the core resource used in the Enlistment Office.

Loyalty

Loyalty represents the support and willingness of the settlement residents to serve the Commander’s army. It is the resource used to recruit troops in the Enlistment Office, and it can be gained through automatic accumulation and daily activities.

  1. Automatic accumulation: The fewer wounded troops there are in the hospital, the faster Loyalty accumulates. The higher your stored Loyalty becomes, the slower the accumulation rate gets.
  2. Daily activities: Claim daily reward chests, gather resources, and obtain materials. The higher the Furnace level, the more Loyalty you gain from daily activities.
