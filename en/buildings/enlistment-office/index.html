Building

Enlistment Office

When wounded troops exceed Hospital capacity, the Enlistment Office recruits survivors from your Settlement to help replenish your army.

Building List Database
← All buildingsRequirements by levelPlanning and useRelated buildings and guides

Planning and use

After a battle, check waiting troops and the amount available for free enlistment. Plan Hospital healing alongside enlistment when preparing for your next battle.

Before upgrading

Check the Recruitment Certificates needed and the troops waiting for enlistment. Review troops awaiting Hospital treatment separately before planning replenishment.
