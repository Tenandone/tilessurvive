Building

Enlistment Office

The Enlistment Office helps replenish troops when wounded troops exceed Hospital capacity. Version 2.6.200 replaced Loyalty with Recruitment Certificates.

Building List Database
← All buildingsRequirements by levelPlanning and useRelated buildings and guides

Planning and use

After combat, check waiting troops and the free recruitment amount in the game. Historical Loyalty values do not predict current recovery amounts.

Before upgrading

The verified building table covers initial construction at Lv.1 only. D resource costs and the current recruitment formula remain unconfirmed; no further upgrade stages are inferred.
