Building

Hero Academy

The Hero Academy produces Skill Books for upgrading hero skills. This home of knowledge and strategy supports the growth of your main heroes.

Building Name
Hero Academy
Max Level
30
Main Effect
Hero Skill Book Production

Planning and use

Check the skill books needed for your main heroes’ next skill upgrades, then include Academy production in your plan.

Before upgrading

Hourly production is not an instant reward. Calculate hero skill costs separately from Academy construction costs.
