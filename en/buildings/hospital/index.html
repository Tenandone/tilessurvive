Hospital

Hospital Rules

These are the main rules to check when operating the Hospital.

Healing and Capacity Guide

  1. Hospital capacity increases as the Hospital level rises.
  2. Only severely wounded troops enter the Hospital for treatment. Lightly wounded troops recover immediately after returning to the base.
  3. Pay close attention to the Hospital’s maximum capacity. If severely wounded troops exceed capacity and cannot be treated, they die immediately.
  4. Severely wounded troops currently under treatment cannot be dismissed.
  5. If free healing remains, only the troops eligible for free healing are shown in the Hospital. After all free healing is used, the remaining troops are then displayed.
  6. Free healing cannot be used in Reservoir Raid.
