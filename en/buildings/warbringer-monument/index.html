Building Detail

Warbringer

Lv.30 Hero Attack Buff

The Warbringer Monument grants an attack buff to heroes. Its golden rifle statue symbolizes strength and the will to fight.

← All buildingsRequirements by levelPlanning and useRelated buildings and guides

Planning and use

For more hero attack, compare the next level’s effect with its resource cost. Plan this separately from hero skill upgrades.

Before upgrading

Hero attack bonuses and building power use different units. Check the effect’s target as well.
