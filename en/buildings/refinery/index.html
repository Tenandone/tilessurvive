Refinery

The Refinery produces Gas. Upgrading increases Gas production speed.

Official representative image. It does not show changes in appearance by level.

← All buildingsRequirements by levelPlanning and useRelated buildings and guides

Planning and use

Check Gas requirements for your target buildings before planning production upgrades. Track Gas separately from Metal production.

Before upgrading

An unconfirmed initial-construction row does not mean free construction. Keep the initial unlock separate from later upgrades.
