Kitchen

Upgrading Kitchen facilities improves food quality and increases survivors’ Satiety.

Official representative image. It does not show changes in appearance by level.

← All buildingsRequirements by levelPlanning and useRelated buildings and guides

Planning and use

Plan Kitchen facilities around survivor Satiety. Houses address sleep quality, while the Greenhouse produces Food; review each need separately.

Before upgrading

Do not use Greenhouse Food production speed as a Kitchen effect. Distinguish building levels from individual facility improvements in the game.
