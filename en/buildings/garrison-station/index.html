Building

Garrison

Troops trained in the Barracks are stationed in the Garrison. Upgrading the Garrison increases troop capacity.

Building List Database
← All buildingsRequirements by levelPlanning and useRelated buildings and guides

Planning and use

Before increasing training in the Barracks, review available Garrison space. Check Hospital space separately when preparing for combat.

Before upgrading

Keep seasonal or skill-based exceptions separate from the base capacity. Verify which conditions apply to your current account.
