Garrison Station

You can fill the Garrison Station with troops through training, and the maximum troop capacity is 1,975,000. When entering a season, if you choose the War Skill Tree instead of the Development Skill Tree, you can keep training without the Garrison Station max limit.

Overview and Operation

Maximum Troop Capacity

The maximum troop capacity of the Garrison Station is 1,975,000.

How Troops Are Added

You can fill the Garrison Station with troops through training.

Season Tip

When entering a season, if you choose the War Skill Tree instead of the Development Skill Tree, you can continue training without the Garrison Station max limit.

Troop Status Sections

The Garrison Station displays separate sections for Base Troops and Wasteland Troops.
