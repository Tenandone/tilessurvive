Barracks

Barracks train the troops that make up your army. Upgrade them to unlock more powerful troops.

Building List Database
← All buildingsRequirements by levelPlanning and useRelated buildings and guides

Planning and use

Start with the requirements of the troop tier you want to train. Review Garrison and Hospital capacity when planning more training.

Before upgrading

Check the barracks number and level required for your chosen troop tier. For initial construction, prepare the required construction item as well as resources.
