Smelter

The Smelter produces Metal. Upgrading increases Metal production speed.

Official representative image. It does not show changes in appearance by level.

← All buildingsRequirements by levelPlanning and useRelated buildings and guides

Planning and use

Use the Metal requirements of your target buildings to plan production. Compare existing Metal with what your resource chests can provide.

Before upgrading

Do not reuse another production building’s costs or output. Check each resource cost in the Smelter configuration you select.
