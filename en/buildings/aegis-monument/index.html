Building Detail

Golden Aegis

Lv.30 Hero Defense Buff

Golden Aegis provides a hero defense buff. Its effect differs from monuments that increase attack or health.

← All buildingsRequirements by levelPlanning and useRelated buildings and guides

Planning and use

Review the next level’s defense bonus when planning durability upgrades. Compare defense and health as separate metrics when considering Guardian of Life.

Before upgrading

The defense effect applies to heroes. Building power is not a damage-reduction percentage.
