Building

Blessed Healing Stele

Blessed Healing Stele increases the chance that allied troops’ severe wounds become light wounds. This combat effect is separate from Hospital capacity.

Building List Database
← All buildingsRequirements by levelPlanning and useRelated buildings and guides

Planning and use

For repeated combat, compare the next level’s light-wound conversion effect and cost. Plan treatment of existing wounded troops separately in the Hospital.

Before upgrading

Increased light-wound conversion is the D effect, separate from Original’s retained Hero Health effect. A conversion chance does not guarantee that every severe wound becomes light.
