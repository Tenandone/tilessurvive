Artifact Workshop

The Artifact Workshop uses transformers to regulate voltage in the power grid. It improves work efficiency and provides a construction-speed buff.

← All buildingsRequirements by levelPlanning and useRelated buildings and guides

Planning and use

If you plan several building upgrades, review the Workshop’s construction-speed effect. Compare the resources and time for its own upgrade with your other building plans.

Before upgrading

Check your active construction-speed effect and the next level’s requirements. Reserve resources for upgrading the Workshop itself.
