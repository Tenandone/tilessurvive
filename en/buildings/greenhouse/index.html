Greenhouse

The Greenhouse produces Food. Upgrading increases Food production speed.

Official representative image. It does not show changes in appearance by level.

← All buildingsRequirements by levelPlanning and useRelated buildings and guides

Planning and use

Review Food needed for upcoming construction when planning production upgrades. Food production and the Kitchen’s survivor Satiety serve different purposes.

Before upgrading

Upgrade resource costs are not production output. Do not infer a payback time where hourly production is unconfirmed.
