Arcadian Conquest · occupation rules

The siege lasts 3 hours. Victory requires 90 minutes of cumulative occupation or the longest total occupation. Four towers remove 2% of garrison troops per strike; intervals decrease from 4 to 1 minute as occupation continues. Arcadia or tower occupation gives 10,000 points per 60 seconds. Rallies support 5 allied teams. Alert-zone settlements relocate 60 minutes before start; relocation is free and Peace Flares disabled from 50 minutes before start to the end. Each Chief receives at most one Governor Pack per event. Troop-tier casualty/kill points and reward quantities are unconfirmed.
