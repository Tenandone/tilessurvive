Rounds & reward conditions

Rounds last 4 hours. At 00:00 UTC daily, 12 Chiefs with similar Power Plant levels are matched. Daily ranking rewards are mailed after settlement when total points exceed zero.

Unclaimed theme rewards go to the bag when the theme ends, except medals. Unclaimed daily-goal rewards are mailed after daily settlement.
