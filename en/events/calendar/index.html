Example: how many units reach 50,000 points?

Alliance Duel · Season 1 · Event day 4 · Hero Development

With 0 existing points, reaching 50,000 points through this action alone requires 4 units: Use a Legendary Hero Fragment. The calculated total is 60,800 points.

Round up to a whole action unit: 50,000 ÷ 15,200 → 4. With 3 units, the total is only 45,600 points.

Only configured base points are calculated. Personal research bonuses and other multipliers are excluded.

Points alone do not unlock a chest or guarantee a reward; confirm unlocks in the game.
