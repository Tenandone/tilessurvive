Featured Talents

Awakening of Thunder

The behemoth core skill Storm of Destruction is upgraded into Ultimate - Storm of Destruction, and damage dealt increases by 1%.

Power of Thunder

After the behemoth uses its core skill, all allied heroes deal 1% more damage for 5 seconds.

Swift Lightning

March capacity increases by 3.0K.

Roar of Thunder

The Shining Griffin skin is unlocked, and behemoth damage increases by an additional 4%.

Order of Thunder

Formation counter advantage increases by 1%.

The material used to activate talents is Active Protein.
