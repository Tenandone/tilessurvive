Featured Talents

Awakening of the Tides

The behemoth core skill Blessing of the Current is upgraded into Ultimate - Blessing of the Current, and healing effect increases by 1%.

Vast Ocean

After the behemoth uses its core skill, all allied heroes take 1% less damage for 5 seconds.

Wave Breaker

March capacity increases by 3.0K.

Law of Poseidon

Formation counter resistance increases by 1%.

Blessing of Poseidon

The Wave Drake skin is unlocked, and behemoth healing increases by an additional 4%.

The material used to activate talents is Active Protein.
