Research order

Build a research order from prerequisites

Separate prerequisite growth and time for your target research

When this appliesGame data to checkRecommended stepsPlanning exampleRelated data and tools

When this applies

Plan after checking laboratory and research unlocks. Seasonal and permanent research do not share an assumed cost table.

Game data to check

The laboratory page links building requirements. The v2.5.500 notice limits Infected Fiend EXP and speedup research bonuses to the successful rally leader.

LabEvent archiveTiles Survive Speedup Calculator

Recommended steps

  1. Check the desired effect and whether your role receives it.
  2. List building and research prerequisites and follow their dependency order.
  3. Consider event points only when that research action qualifies.

Planning example

If you only join rallies, do not count leader-only bonuses as your own reward increase. Check the role condition before choosing the next research.
