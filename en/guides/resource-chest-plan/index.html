Choose a reward for the resource you currently lack

When this applies

Resource Choice Chests Lv.1/Lv.2 select one option. Festive Chests showing Party Cake use a separate reward-preview probability table.

Game data to check

Do not sum all four options as simultaneous rewards. The festive preview shows 10 items at 10%, 3 at 30% and 1 at 60%; it does not guarantee availability or server draws.

Lv. 1 Resources Custom CrateLv. 2 Resources Custom CrateFestive ChestTiles Survive Building List

Recommended steps

  1. Calculate each resource shortage for the next build or research.
  2. Check chest level and the quantity for one selected option.
  3. Do not budget preview probabilities as guaranteed income.

Planning example

Selecting food from one chest does not also grant that chest’s wood, metal and gas. Subtract only the chosen resource from your shortage.
