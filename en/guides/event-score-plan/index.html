Calculate Arms Race points before spending

Compare actions and spending needed for a reward threshold

When this appliesGame data to checkRecommended stepsPlanning exampleRelated data and tools

When this applies

These hero-development rules were displayed on 2026-10-10 for competition Lv.30–30. Do not mix them with troop-training points or another bracket.

Game data to check

One legendary fragment gives 9,000 points, epic 1,000 and rare 150. One manual used to upgrade a hero skill gives 857; one diamond obtained from a bundle purchase gives 60.

Recommended steps

  1. Match the current theme, competition level and chest threshold to your screen.
  2. Calculate planned hero upgrades first, then compare the remaining amount.
  3. We recommend staying within your budget rather than buying solely for points.

Planning example

Using one legendary fragment and ten manuals under those conditions gives 9,000 + 10 × 857 = 17,570 points. This is a planning example, not a payout prediction.
