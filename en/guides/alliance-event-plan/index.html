Alliance and co-op

Align personal spending with alliance goals

Coordinate roles, points and troop recovery with your alliance

When this applies

Alliance Duel, Reservoir Raid and Arcadian Conquest have different rules. Check eligibility and dates for the selected activity first.

Game data to check

Pet-unlocked servers have pet-development tasks on Alliance Duel day 2 (v2.6.200). Arcadian contest duration is 2 hours from v2.5.600; check the versions of occupation and reward rules separately.

Recommended steps

  1. Align eligibility and your personal goal with the alliance schedule.
  2. Assign attack, defense and support roles with speedup and recovery budgets.
  3. Count overlapping points only when the same action actually qualifies in both events.

Planning example

Scheduling pet training on day 2 is a recommendation. The notice gives no alliance-point rate per imprint; check the displayed points before budgeting training.
