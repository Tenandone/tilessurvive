Gear and exclusive weapons

Separate materials by upgrade type

Prepare gear, reforging and exclusive-weapon costs without mixing them

When this appliesGame data to checkRecommended stepsPlanning exampleRelated data and tools

When this applies

Match quality and target level. Effect comparisons and upgrade costs are separate; do not infer a cost from an effect-only table.

Game data to check

The site separates gear EXP, reforging, legendary gear stars and exclusive gear. The 390-fragment limit in v2.4.100 is per exclusive weapon and accounts for prior exchanges.

Recommended steps

  1. Identify the equipment type and quality first.
  2. Calculate materials over the target range and subtract inventory.
  3. Compare exchanges only after checking the remaining limit and actual currency.

Planning example

390 is not a price. After 100 prior exchanges, the notice’s limit leaves 290 fragments; availability and payment currency still follow the shop screen.
