When this applies

Use the table matching your building and server conditions. Do not mix normal, seasonal or alternate cost profiles.

Game data to check

Resource cost, time and prerequisites are separate table fields. Speedup items reduce time; they do not replace missing construction materials.

Recommended steps

  1. Select current and target levels, then check prerequisite buildings.
  2. Subtract inventory from the required total for each resource.
  3. Use the time calculator to compare remaining time with speedups.

Planning example

With 60 minutes remaining and ten 5-minute speedups, you have 50 minutes and a 10-minute gap. Construction resources must still be covered separately.
