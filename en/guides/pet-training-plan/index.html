Pets and Behemoths

Plan pet EXP and training imprints separately

Calculate missing imprints for the skill unlock stage you need

When this appliesGame data to checkRecommended stepsPlanning exampleRelated data and tools

When this applies

Select the pet’s own imprint. The training calculator totals completed-stage boundaries, not partial progress within a stage.

Game data to check

Seven pet EXP and training profiles are separate. Published level-1 skills for six pets distinguish the basic skill from training-stage 2, 4 and 5 unlocks.

Pet directoryDodoTiles Survive Behemoth List

Recommended steps

  1. Select the pet and check the desired skill’s unlock stage.
  2. Enter completed current and target stages plus owned imprints.
  3. Keep EXP food and training imprints separate; use separate Behemoth material tables.

Planning example

Dodo’s completed stages 2→5 cost 1,700 imprints. With 80 owned, the shortage is 1,620. Do not reuse this cost for another pet.
