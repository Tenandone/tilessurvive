What is LootBar?

An external marketplace for game top-ups. TilesSurvive.net is an independent guide and does not process orders.

How is it different from buying in the game?

The price, payment and delivery follow LootBar's offer. Compare the same pack and the final payable amount.

What is Self-TopUp?

LootBar describes a flow where you sign in to the game through its interface and redeem your purchased pack.

What are the steps?

Check the pack and eligibility, pay on LootBar, follow Self-TopUp, then verify delivery in the game.

Which countries and payments work?

Check your region and server at checkout. LootBar lists Visa, debit cards and digital wallets; availability varies.

What account information is needed?

LootBar's current process may require a LootBar account and a game login. Follow LootBar's current login instructions. This site never asks for your game password, verification code or payment details.

Who handles a problem?

Contact LootBar support with the order number and an error screenshot. Never send a password or verification code to support.
