PRIVACY

Site usage statistics and privacy

We apply the same privacy-minimizing measurement settings across all 21 languages. Guides and tools do not require registration, a real name or contact details.

Last updated 2026-11-06

What we measure

GA4 (G-Z2MJ6TXZHK) measures page views, language, approximate country, tool use, gift-code copies and TEN link clicks. Events use an explicit allowlist. Measurement parameters exclude raw URL queries, fragments, calculator or form inputs, and full external URLs.

Cookieless signals

Analytics and advertising consent states default to denied and are not automatically granted. GA4 can still send cookieless signals, including technical request information. The site does not create individual visitor profiles or its own persistent analytics identifiers.

IP address processing

Google and hosting infrastructure process IP addresses to handle requests. Google states that EU, Swiss and UK IP addresses are discarded after deriving location. Elsewhere, Google uses IP addresses for basic services; linked advertising services may also use encrypted IP data under their settings. Cookieless does not mean no data is transmitted or processed.

Stop or resume transmission

The controls below stop or resume this site's GA4 transmission in this browser. The preference is stored only here and does not inherit previous consent records. Resuming never changes consent to granted. Transmission remains stopped while GPC or DNT is enabled. These controls do not change advertising consent or your saved tool plans.

Google and other service providers

Search Console provides aggregated Google Search performance, separate from on-site usage. GA4, GitHub Pages and AdSense continue to process data under their respective terms. Required advertising consent and CMP controls remain available. This notice does not describe the entire site or these providers as cookie-free.

What the numbers mean

Counts and modeled estimates are not an exact count of unique visitors. Blocking, network failures and reporting methods can create gaps. A TEN click is not proof of a purchase. We do not invent missing historical statistics.
