    FAQ

    Is this event helper an official tool?

    No. It is a fan-made tool organized by TilesSurvive.net and is not officially operated by the game publisher.

    What does ?needs verification? mean?

    It means the data has not been fully checked against the latest live in-game schedule. Confirm details in game before spending resources.

    Why is there a LootBar link?

    It is an informational recommendation for players who want to check top-up benefits while preparing for events. It is placed as a restrained CTA.
